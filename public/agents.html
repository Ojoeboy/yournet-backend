Agents

Agents log in on their own at /agent.html to see their own sales/commission, and (once they've set up a secret question) generate and print their own vouchers, capped at 100/day.

Add an agent

The secret question protects the agent's own voucher-generation access - it's asked once per login before they can generate, so a stolen password alone isn't enough.

Activity Log

Every agent-self-generated voucher batch, plus any wrong/locked secret-question attempts, most recent first.
