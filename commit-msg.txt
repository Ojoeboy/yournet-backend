Enforce wall-clock voucher expiry regardless of router uptime accounting

Problem: MikroTik's limit-uptime (and equivalent per-platform grants)
count cumulative CONNECTED time, not wall-clock time - a session pauses
on disconnect and resumes on reconnect. So a customer with any
connection gaps could stay authorized on the router well past the
24-hour mark they were actually sold, while the backend's expires_at
field (set once at redemption, wall-clock only) already showed the
voucher as over. Nothing previously enforced expires_at against the
router - it was a display/record value only, per the existing
delete-guard comment in routes/vouchers.js ("expires_at, not status, is
what actually tells you the session is over").

- New services/voucherExpiry.js: runs every minute, finds vouchers
  where status='active' AND expires_at <= now(), groups by site, and
  actively kicks/revokes them on the router:
    - mikrotik: removeHotspotUser (kicks active session + deletes
      router user in one call)
    - unifi: unauthorizeClient using the voucher's client_mac, when one
      was captured at redemption
    - omada/meraki: no revoke primitive exists yet for either platform
      in integrations/*.js, so these are left untouched and logged
      rather than marking 'expired' while the customer might still be
      connected - avoids reintroducing the same status/reality mismatch
      for site types this pass can't actually enforce
  On successful revoke, flips status to 'expired', so the DB stops
  drifting from actual router state (fixes the mismatch the delete-guard
  comment flagged).

- server.js: wired the sweep in on a 1-minute interval, same
  setInterval/setTimeout-on-boot pattern as the existing
  subscriptionBilling/pppoeBilling jobs. Tighter interval than those
  since lateness here directly means a customer stays online longer
  than they paid for.

Net effect: "buy 24 hours" now means 24 wall-clock hours from
redemption on mikrotik/unifi sites, independent of usage gaps. Known
gap: omada/meraki sites aren't enforced by this pass yet.
