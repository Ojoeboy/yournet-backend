Fix flash of protected content before auth redirect

Both /admin and /license-admin only redirected unauthenticated
visitors in a <script> near the end of <body>, by which point the
browser had already painted the whole protected page (setup wizard /
license key list) - a flash of real content before the redirect fired.

Added a synchronous check at the very top of <head> on both pages
that redirects immediately if the relevant token isn't present, before
anything below is parsed or painted. The original checks stay in place
further down as a safety net (and because admin.html's relies on it to
set the token variable used elsewhere).
