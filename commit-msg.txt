Fix Gmail SMTP connection timeout by forcing IPv4 and explicit host/port

- getTransporter() in src/integrations/emailService.js now connects to
  smtp.gmail.com:465 explicitly with family:4, instead of the 'gmail'
  shorthand
- Render's containers resolve smtp.gmail.com to IPv6 by default, and
  that connection hangs until timeout ("Connection timeout" in logs) -
  the 'service: gmail' shorthand gave no way to force IPv4
- Also sets connectionTimeout:15000 so a real failure surfaces in
  ~15s instead of nodemailer's much longer default
