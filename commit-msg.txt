Make manual MoMo a permanent option, add owner alerts on new claims

Manual pay used to disappear once a gateway was configured. Now it
stays as a second, permanent option - some buyers prefer sending
directly (no gateway fees, no third-party checkout page) over
automated checkout, even once one exists. Wording adjusts depending
on which options are actually available, so buyers who care about
speed know the automated option is instant and this one isn't.

- license.html: manual card no longer hides itself when a gateway is
  configured; renders different copy depending on whether one exists
- brevo.js: new sendManualClaimAlertEmail() - notifies OWNER_ALERT_EMAIL
  (separate from OWNER_MOMO_NUMBER/NAME, which is what buyers see, not
  where alerts go)
- license.js: /purchase/claim-manual now fires that email plus an SMS
  to OWNER_ALERT_PHONE (via the existing sendSms) the moment a claim
  comes in - both best-effort, a failed alert doesn't fail the buyer's
  submission since the claim is already saved regardless
- .env.example: documents OWNER_ALERT_EMAIL / OWNER_ALERT_PHONE

Keeping this permanently is a real ongoing tradeoff, not just a
temporary stopgap anymore - every claim still needs a human to check
it against their own MoMo message before approving. These alerts are
what makes that sustainable instead of requiring /license-admin to be
checked on a timer.
