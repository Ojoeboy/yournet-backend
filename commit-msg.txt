Bump service worker cache version to v3->v4

sw.js's CACHE_NAME never changed after the shell.js sidebar update,
so browsers that already had the service worker installed kept
serving the old cached shell.js/shell.css - meaning the new owner
sidebar wouldn't show up until the cache was manually cleared.

Bumping CACHE_NAME forces a reinstall on next visit, which purges
the stale cache (see the activate handler) and pulls the current
shell.js/shell.css from the network.

One-line change, public/sw.js only.
